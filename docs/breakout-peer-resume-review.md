# Breakout: Peer Resume Review (ungraded)

Bring a resume draft if you have one. If you do not, bring a blank document and the jobs, classes, projects, tools, and software you would put on it.

We'll spend twenty minutes in pairs. Swap. Each person names one thing that is unclear and one thing that is missing. That's the whole exercise. Not graded, and you leave with two concrete edits to make.
