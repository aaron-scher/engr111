# Conversation 1: Meet an Adviser

Meet with an academic adviser and leave with a term-by-term plan that gets you to transfer.

**Book early.** Advisers fill up around registration. Schedule through Navigate in your student portal or email advising@clackamas.edu and ask for the STEM adviser. In person, phone or Zoom all count. A transfer adviser at the school you're aiming for also counts.

Bring your unofficial transcript. The point of the meeting is to come out knowing what to take next term and the term after, which math and physics sequence you're on, and what your target school needs for admission into the engineering major.

**Submit:** who you met and when, a photo or screenshot of the plan you built together with course numbers and terms on it, and two or three sentences on what you didn't know before the meeting. Type it into the Moodle submission box; the plan can be pasted in as an image or attached as a file.

**Grading.** No meeting, no credit. Otherwise, grading and the AI policy are in the syllabus.
