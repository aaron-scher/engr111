# Final Paper: Design Your Process for Becoming a World-Class Engineering Student

This paper is your plan for how you will work as an engineering student.

For each item on the list below, tell me three things:

- Where a world-class engineering student would be on it
- Where you actually are
- What you'll do to close the gap

The third is the main part.

This draws on your earlier homework and on your adviser meeting. Much of the material is already in those.

Minimum five full pages, 1.5 spacing, 12 point, 1 inch margins. Submit one PDF. Two sample papers from previous students: [sample 1](files/sample-final-paper-1.pdf) and [sample 2](files/sample-final-paper-2.pdf). The original version of this assignment is Appendix A of your textbook if you want to see where it comes from.

**The items**

*Goal setting*

1. Setting your goals: major, time to graduation, GPA
2. Strengthening and clarifying your commitment to your goals
3. Setting up a road map: a plan to guide you over the next years to graduation. This is the plan from your adviser meeting
4. Understanding what the field of engineering looks like and how you might fit in it, based on your interests

*Community*

5. Building relationships, and making effective use of your peers
6. Participating in co-curricular activities

*Academic development*

7. Navigating the community college and university system, resources, and academic advising
8. Understanding teaching styles and modes of learning, and making the teaching and learning process work for you

*Personal development*

9. Enhancing your self-awareness and improving your skills at practicing academic success strategies
10. Outlining what attitudes and behaviors you need to change or add to be successful
11. Managing time and tasks
12. Engaging in good health and wellness practices, including managing stress
13. Developing a high sense of personal and professional integrity and ethical behavior

Then, if there is something not on this list that matters for your success, add it as item 14.

**How it's graded**

| Criterion | Full | Most | Some | Little |
|:----------------------------|:----------------------|:-------------------|:-------------------|:----------------|
| **Every item, all three parts** (50) | All 13 items, each with all three parts | One or two items thin or missing a part | Several items skipped or reduced to a sentence | Most items missing |
| **Plan quality** (25) | Each "what you'll do" is specific: an action and when | Specific in most places | General intentions | No plan |
| **About you** (15) | Draws on your homework, your adviser meeting, your real classes and habits | Partly generic | Could have been written by anyone | Not about you |
| **Length, format, readability** (10) | Meets the length and format, reads clearly | Slightly short or unclear in places | Well short, or hard to follow | Both |
