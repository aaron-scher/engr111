# Project: Topic

One paragraph, and it's not binding. Tell me:

- What you're thinking about building
- What problem it solves, or what need it meets
- Roughly what it's made of and roughly what it costs

Three or four sentences is enough. If you have two ideas and can't choose, describe both and say which way you're leaning. If you have no idea at all, say that and tell me what you're interested in. That's a useful answer and I'll write back with suggestions.

You can change your topic later. The point of this is that I find out early enough to help.

I will reply to each one with feedback: go ahead, this is too big and here is what to cut, or come talk to me. Projects most often fail because they were too ambitious from the start; this paragraph is where that gets caught.

Type it into the Moodle submission box.

**Grading.** Full credit for a real paragraph, on time. A late topic still gets my reply. Otherwise, grading and the AI policy are in the syllabus.
