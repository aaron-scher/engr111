## Three errors in the book

- **Page 65** says the discipline descriptions are in "Appendix D." They are in **Appendix E**. 
- **The second key to success** is "Approach: work smart" on p.7 and "Approach: work efficiently" on p.19. Same edition, two wordings. Lectures use p.19.
- **The NASA box in 6.6** (p.205) calls the Mars Climate Orbiter a "Mars Rover." It was an orbiter, lost before orbital insertion. Lecture 9 gets it right.
