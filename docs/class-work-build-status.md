# In-Class Work 7: Build Status and Risk

**We do this live in class.** Small breakout rooms. This is the second check on your project, same format as In-Class Work 6. By now you should have part of your project built or running.

Each person gets about eight minutes:

1. Show your project as it is today, in about two minutes. Hold it up to the camera, share your screen, show the code, show the test data. Something concrete, not a description
2. Say what is blocking you: a part that has not arrived, a step that is not working, something you do not know how to do
3. The group gives one specific suggestion for the blocker
4. The group answers: can this project finish before presentations? If not, agree on what to cut

**One person posts for the group** to the In-Class Work 7 forum:

- Subject line: your room number
- First line: everyone's full names
- For each person, starting with their name: what they showed, what is blocking them, and the group's suggestion
- For anyone the group thinks will not finish in time: what you agreed to cut

**Grading.** Everyone named in the post gets the same score. If your name is not in a post, you get no credit for that session, so check the names before you post. Grading and the AI policy are in the syllabus. Specific to this one: step 4 is the point. An honest "this will not finish, cut this part" is worth more than a polite "looks good."
