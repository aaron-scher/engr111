# Class Work 7: Build Status and Risk (Studio 2)

**We do this live in class.** Breakout rooms of four. Same format as Studio 1, different questions, and by now everyone should have something physical or something running.

Each person gets about eight minutes:

1. Show what you have. Hold it up to the camera, share your screen, show the code, show the spreadsheet. Something concrete
2. Say what's blocked
3. The group gives one specific suggestion. Not encouragement, a suggestion
4. The group answers: **can this finish before presentations?** If the honest answer is no, say what should be cut

**One person posts for the group** to the Class Work 7 forum:

- Subject line: your room number
- First line: everyone's full names
- For each person: what they showed, what's blocked, and the group's suggestion
- Flag anyone the group thinks won't finish, and what you told them to cut

**Grading.** Everyone named in the post gets the same score. If your name is not in a post, you get no credit for that session, so check the names before you post. Grading and the AI policy are in the syllabus. Step 4 is the point. Telling a classmate honestly that their project is too big is doing them a favor, and it's what I'd want a colleague to tell me.
